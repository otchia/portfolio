e-RoomReserve

AI-Driven Smart Campus Space Optimization System

A smarter way to find, reserve, and manage campus rooms. e-RoomReserve brings room booking and occupancy monitoring together, using BLE beacon technology to help make room use more visible, reliable, and efficient at St. Dominic College of Asia.

What we set out to do

The objectives

Seven goals guiding the design, development, and evaluation of e-RoomReserve.

Study aim

Design and develop an AI-integrated room reservation and occupancy monitoring system using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacon technology to improve space utilization, enforce reservation policies, and support efficient facility management at St. Dominic College of Asia.

01

Make reservations paperless

Design a mobile and web application for room requests, bulk scheduling of recurring classes, faculty approvals, reservation tracking, and user notifications. Provide tailored interfaces for students, faculty, utility staff, building administrators, and super administrators.

02

Verify presence with BLE

Use the ESP32 BLE Beacon Module to confirm a user is near a room before a reservation activates, monitor occupancy in real time, and mark a room vacant when the registered device leaves beacon range after a configurable timeout.

03

Alert staff when attention is needed

Notify staff about BLE signal loss during an active booking, occupancy past the scheduled end time, and cases that need physical inspection as a fallback verification step.

04

Improve reservation control

Reduce scheduling conflicts, unverified room use, and administrative workload through faculty-verified check-in, role-based access, and automated conflict detection.

05

Recommend spaces and learn from feedback

Suggest alternative rooms using capacity, amenities, and size; link event-related reservations to DC Space for cross-system event tracking; and analyze post-reservation feedback by gender to inform facility maintenance.

06

Turn usage into useful insight

Analyze room-use logs, occupancy data, and survey responses to help faculty and administrators improve scheduling, enforce policies, and manage facilities.

07

Evaluate the system

Assess performance, usability, and effectiveness through unit testing, integration testing, and user acceptance testing in the St. Dominic College of Asia context.
